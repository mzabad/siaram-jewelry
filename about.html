About Siaram

Where It Started

Simply elegant, endlessly yours

Siaram was born from a love of the pieces we reach for every single day — the necklace that never comes off, the ring you forget you're wearing because it feels like part of your hand.

We design jewelry meant to be layered, mixed, and lived in. Nothing precious enough to be locked away for special occasions — every piece is made to go from morning coffee to dinner out without a second thought.

Craftsmanship

Made with care, made to last

Each design starts with a simple sketch and goes through several rounds of refinement before it becomes a finished piece. We work in gold and hand-select every stone, favoring quality and comfort over trend.

Because these are pieces meant for daily wear, durability matters as much as design — every clasp, chain, and setting is checked before it reaches you.

Designed to Layer

Every new piece is designed to sit naturally alongside what you already own.

Small Batches
